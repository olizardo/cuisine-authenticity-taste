## Why this works for your specific model:
Your model is evaluating an Adjacent Category (`acat`) likelihood over a massive number of rows in `dat_long` (since each respondent has 15 rows for the 15 cuisines). 
Currently, each MCMC chain uses exactly 1 CPU core to sequentially calculate the likelihood of every single row for every iteration. Because you have a 12-core server but are only running 4 chains, you are leaving **8 CPU cores entirely idle** during sampling.

## The Solution: Within-Chain Threading
By utilizing CmdStan's `threading()` capability, we can tell `brms` to split the massive dataset into chunks and calculate the likelihoods in parallel *within* each chain.

If we assign **2 or 3 threads per chain**:
- 4 chains × 3 threads = 12 total threads.
- This will perfectly saturate your 12-core server. 
- It essentially divides the heavy lifting of the likelihood calculations by 3, which can yield a **2x to 3x speedup on top of everything else we've already done.**
