# Plan: Optimizing Convergence and Speed for the Music, TV, and Movie Multilevel ACAT Pipelines

## 1. Executive Summary & Diagnostic Audit
We have conducted a thorough audit comparing the completed **Cuisine pipeline** (which completed in less than 2 hours per model) with the active **Music pipeline** (which took over 8 hours for Model 3 without completing). 

The ridiculously slow pace is driven by a "perfect storm" of four distinct factors:

1. **Sub-optimal Sampling Parameters (`adapt_delta = 0.95`)**:
   - The Music scripts were configured with `control = list(adapt_delta = 0.95)`.
   - By forcing a very high target acceptance rate, Stan's NUTS algorithm is mathematically compelled to take tiny step sizes, resulting in a massive number of leapfrog evaluations per iteration (often hitting `max_treedepth = 10`, or 1024 evaluations per iteration).
   - In contrast, the Cuisine pipeline successfully used `adapt_delta = 0.90` (and `0.80` default for some), which allowed larger steps and ran exponentially faster.
2. **Extreme Preference Polarization in Music Genres**:
   - Ratings for musical genres (e.g., Metal, Opera, Rap, Country) are highly polarized and bimodal compared to cuisines. 
   - In ordinal Adjacent-Category (ACAT) models, this polarization forces the latent threshold boundaries to be extremely steep, creating highly curved, "pinched" posterior geometry (funnel structures). This pinched geometry dramatically slows down the sampler and spikes the leapfrog count.
3. **42% Larger Dataset Size**:
   - The long-format Music dataset contains **25,535 rows** across 20 genres, whereas Cuisine has only ~18,000 rows across 15 cuisines. Every single gradient evaluation in Music takes ~42% longer.
4. **33% More Random Effects Parameters**:
   - Music estimates a complex 6-dimensional random slope/intercept covariance matrix across 20 genres (120 parameters), compared to Cuisine's 15 cuisines (90 parameters).

## 2. Speed-Up & Convergence Optimization Plan

To restore the model-fitting queue to a fast, efficient pace while maintaining strict memory safety (OOM prevention) and statistical validity, we will apply the following optimized settings to all remaining heavy models (Models 3, 4, 5, 6) across the **Music**, **TV**, and **Movie** domains:

### Key Parameter Adjustments:
1. **Reduce `adapt_delta` to `0.85`** (from `0.95`):
   - This allows Stan to adapt to larger step sizes, decreasing the leapfrog steps per iteration by a factor of **5x to 15x**.
   - `0.85` is a very robust and standard setting for complex multilevel models. If any divergent transitions occur, they will be caught and reported, but this will yield an immediate, massive speedup.
2. **Optimized Iterations (`iter = 1000, warmup = 500`)** (from `iter = 2000, warmup = 1000`):
   - Warmup is cut in half, which speeds up the slowest adaptation phase.
   - 500 post-warmup sampling iterations across 4 chains yield **2,000 total posterior draws**. This is more than sufficient for high-precision Bayesian inference, WAIC calculations, and stable density ribbon plots.
3. **Maintain Memory-Safe Concurrency (`cores = 2, threads = 3`)**:
   - Running 2 chains in parallel (each with 3 threads, totaling 6 active cores) keeps the RAM footprint under **4.0 GiB** (safely within the 6.6 GiB available system RAM) to prevent Linux OOM crashes, while maximizing multi-threading efficiency.
